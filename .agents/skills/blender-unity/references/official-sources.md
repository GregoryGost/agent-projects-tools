## Source precedence

1. Target project's Unity version/packages/importer presets and existing assets.
2. Exact Blender 5.2 and Unity 6000.3 official docs.
3. This skill's portable guidance.
4. Community/DCC recipes only after checking they do not duplicate axis/scale/import conversions.
